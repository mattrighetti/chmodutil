## Generation prompt

Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: production app icon for chmod util, a minimal native Unix permissions calculator for iPhone and Mac. Create one brand-new polished square 1024x1024 icon. The design is an extremely simple, confident typographic mark: the exact lowercase text "rwx" in a beautifully drawn bold monospaced typeface, centered with generous negative space. The three letters should be immediately legible at small icon sizes. Use a restrained neutral monochrome treatment with clear contrast and subtle depth in the full-bleed background, consistent with a clean minimal developer utility. Flat frontal composition, impeccable letter spacing and optical centering. No extra words, no padlock, no shields, no devices, no decorative badges, no busy illustration, no border, no watermark, no surrounding canvas or mockup. The entire square is the icon artwork: fill to all four edges, keep square corners because the OS supplies its own mask. Fully opaque background. Exact text: r w x, joined as "rwx".
